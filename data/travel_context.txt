Indore: Known for Rajwada Palace, Sarafa Bazaar, and street food. 
Best time to visit is from October to March. Budget friendly for students.

Manali: Famous for snow, adventure sports, and beautiful mountain views.
Ideal months are December to February. Medium to high budget required.

Jaipur: The Pink City known for Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, and heritage culture.
Best for couples and culture lovers. Travel between November to February.

Goa: Best for beaches, parties, and sunset views. Low to high budget trips available.
Best time is from November to March.

Lonavala: Hill station near Mumbai. Best to visit during monsoon (July–September).
Ideal for nature and peace lovers.
